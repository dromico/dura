Deluxe Commercial Chips Tile

A classic chips tile, asbestos free, in eleven designs.

Enquire about this range Download PDF (0.4 MB)
Rendered with DX1109, at true size

Eleven designs

With a reputation for being both practical and economical, Deluxe commercial chips tile is classic floor material. Made of an environmentally-friendly vinyl compound with no asbestos, this floor tile comes in a convenient dimension (300mm x 300mm) and is extremely practical due to its durability and fire-resistant qualities. This tile is easy to clean and easy to maintain, with almost no deterioration of colour or pattern, even in tough and abrasive conditions.

Description

Size300mm x 300mm (Asbestos Free)
Thickness2.0mm
Packing55 pieces / carton
Weight21.7kg / carton
SpecificationManufacture in accordance with American Standard (SGS with E662)
Resistance toCooking Oil, Gasoline, Kerosene, Cottonseed Oil, Sodium Hydroxide 2%, Cement Paste

Installation

Subfloor should be smooth, level, dry, free from dust, oil and water. Use appropriate adhesive recommended by distributor.

Maintenance

Once the floor is installed, wipe thoroughly with a damp mop and apply a layer of seal to protect the surface. Daily maintenance is by damp mopping whilst regular cleaning or maintenance schedule should be established based on your specific traffic.

Recommended Application

School, Health Care, Public Buildings, Industries, Restaurants, Supermarkets, Retail Outlets and Offices.
