Description

Size304.8mm x 304.8mm (Asbestos Free)
Thickness2.0mm
Packing50 pieces / carton
Weight18.8kg / carton
SpecificationManufacture in accordance with Japanese Industrial Standard (JLS 5705 A OF 1990)
Resistance toCooking Oil, Gasoline, Kerosene, Cottonseed Oil, Sodium Hydroxide 2%, Cement Paste

Installation

Subfloor should be smooth, level, dry, free from dust, oil and water. Use appropriate adhesive recommended by distributor.

Maintenance

Once the floor is installed, wipe thoroughly with a damp mop and apply a layer of seal to protect the surface. Daily maintenance is by damp mopping whilst regular cleaning or maintenance schedule should be established based on your specific traffic.

Recommended Application

School, Health Care, Public Buildings, Industries, Restaurants, Supermarkets, Retail Outlets and Offices.
